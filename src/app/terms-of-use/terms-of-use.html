1. About TPLCounsel

TPLCounsel is an educational and public legal awareness platform dedicated to making Indian laws easily accessible to everyone. The Website provides information relating to various civil and criminal laws, including but not limited to:

  • Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
  • Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
  • Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)
  • Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Indian Trusts Act, 1882
  • Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Other Central Acts and legal resources
  • Judicial decisions and detailed legal case studies
  • Legal notes, explanations, and educational materials

In addition to legal education, TPLCounsel operates a Free Legal Consultancy Team that strives to provide preliminary legal guidance to economically weaker, underprivileged, and needy individuals.

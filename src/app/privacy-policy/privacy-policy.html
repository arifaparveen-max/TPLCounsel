Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

TPLCounsel (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of visitors and users of our Website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact us or request free legal consultation, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Postal address (if necessary)
  • Facts relating to your legal issue
  • Documents voluntarily submitted for review
  • Any other information you choose to share

You should provide only the information necessary for us to understand your legal query.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website

This information helps us improve Website performance and security.

2. Free Legal Consultancy

Information submitted for free legal consultation is used solely for:

  • Understanding your legal issue
  • Responding to your legal query
  • Connecting you with an appropriate legal volunteer or advocate, where applicable
  • Maintaining records of consultations for administrative purposes

Submitting a consultation request does not create an advocate-client relationship.

TPLCounsel treats consultation requests with reasonable confidentiality. However, users are requested not to submit highly sensitive personal or financial information unless specifically requested by the legal professional handling the matter.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Provide free legal guidance
  • Improve educational content
  • Improve Website functionality
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information only:

  • With your consent
  • With advocates or legal volunteers involved in providing the requested consultation
  • Where required by law
  • To comply with judicial or governmental directions
  • To protect legal rights, safety, or prevent fraud

5. Cookies

The Website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience and analyze Website traffic. Users may disable cookies through their browser settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to safeguard the information we receive. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention of Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your query, provide legal consultation, maintain administrative records, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Website is intended as an educational resource for the general public. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to official government websites, courts, or other external resources. TPLCounsel is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will become effective upon publication on this page.

11. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or the free legal consultancy service, please contact TPLCounsel through the contact details available on the Website.
